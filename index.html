The Fold · A floral studio in Stratford-upon-Avon

Why seasonal?

Around 85% of flowers sold in Britain are imported. Ours aren't.

Global supply lines are getting longer, costlier and more fragile. We think the answer is growing closer to home: supporting British flower farming, cutting air freight and plastics out of the process, and matching each occasion to what the season does best — not forcing June flowers into February.

The Fold philosophy

Nature isn't imperfect.

It's weathered.
Seasonal.
Ephemeral.
Unexpected.
Transient.
Unrepeatable.

It's about acceptance — not settling, not "making do". Acceptance in the sense of seeing something exactly as it is and finding beauty there. The bent stem. The petal marked by rain. The blossom that came a week early. The cow parsley that disappears because June was too dry.

Shapes wander. Stems bend. Petals bruise. Blossom comes and goes in its own time. The result is work that feels alive, generous and deeply connected to the landscape it came from — because the most beautiful arrangements aren't those that strive for perfection, but those that leave room for nature to tell part of the story.

Questions

Asked often

Do you deliver wedding flowers outside Stratford-upon-Avon?

Yes — we work with couples across Warwickshire, Worcestershire and the Cotswolds, and travel to venues throughout the region.

Are your flowers really British grown?

The majority of our stems come from Anna Brown's family-run flower farm in Wardington, Oxfordshire, supplemented by a small network of verified British growers. In deep winter we're honest about what Britain can and cannot grow — and design accordingly.

Do I need experience to join a workshop?

None at all. Workshops are designed for complete beginners as well as returning flower lovers, and every session ends with an arrangement you take home.

Can I really make my own wedding flowers?

Yes — the DIY wedding flowers programme covers conditioning, arranging and flower care across one or more sessions, with stems supplied by our growers the week of your wedding.
