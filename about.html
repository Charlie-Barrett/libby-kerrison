Who we are

Trained in Mayfair. Rooted in the Midlands.

We're a small floristry studio in the heart of Stratford-upon-Avon with an outsized conviction: that the British floristry industry — the growers, the seasons, the craft — is one of the country's most overlooked treasures, and it's time it got the stage.

Where we learned

The pedigree behind the petals

Training

McQueens Flower School, Mayfair

Members of our team trained at McQueens — the London school whose alumni shape floristry worldwide. It's where technique became second nature, so seasonality could become the focus.

Experience

Daylesford Organic

Time with the back-of-house floristry team at Daylesford taught us what flowers look like when farming, seasonality and design live under one roof — the model this studio is built on.

Teaching

Educators first

With a background in teaching, sharing the craft is in this studio's DNA — from beginner workshops to brides making their own wedding flowers.

What drives us

The right flower, the right moment

The Fold celebrates flowers as they are — seasonal, fleeting and beautifully individual. The character of a flower comes not from perfect petals, but from the landscape that grew it, the weather that shaped it and the moment in which it was gathered. Flowers don't bloom to a schedule: every season unfolds in its own time, shaped by rain, sunshine, cold mornings and warm evenings, and we work with nature rather than asking it to be something else. Every arrangement tells the story of the season that created it.

That's also why we teach. Every person who leaves a workshop knowing what grows here, and when, is another voice for British flowers.
