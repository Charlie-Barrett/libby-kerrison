Dolphin House Arts Centre · Stratford-upon-Avon

Floristry workshops that follow the seasons.

Small-group flower arranging classes with British-grown stems, guided by a McQueens-trained team. The flowers change with the calendar — so no two terms are ever the same. Complete beginners warmly welcome.

The workshop year

Choose your season

Every programme is built around what our growers are cutting. Pick a season to see what a class looks like.

Spring — bulbs, blossom & beginnings

Tulips, narcissi, ranunculus and flowering branches. Learn spiral hand-ties and how to let spring stems keep their natural bend rather than fighting it.

  • Hand-tied spring bouquet class
  • Mother's Day gathering workshop
  • Easter table & branch arranging

Spring — tulips & narcissi

Summer — the meadow months

The British year at full volume: cornflowers, sweet peas, achillea, cosmos and dahlias arriving late summer. Meadow-style bowls, garden-gathered hand-ties, and colour theory straight from the field.

  • Meadow bowl masterclass — foam-free mechanics
  • Garden hand-tie evening classes
  • Flower crowns & hen party sessions (private bookings)
Register interest
Summer — dahlias & cosmos

Autumn — texture & harvest

Dahlias at their peak, grasses, seed heads, hips and foliage turning. Classes lean into texture: asymmetric arrangements, dried-flower work and preserving the season for winter.

  • Late dahlia abundance workshop
  • Dried flower & seed head arranging
  • Harvest table styling
Register interest
Autumn — dahlias, grasses, hips

Winter — wreaths, branches & stillness

The quiet, sculptural end of the year: foraged evergreens, hellebores, forced bulbs and branch work. December brings our Christmas wreath-making workshops — the best-loved classes of the year, on moss bases with everything compostable.

  • Christmas wreath making (December — book early, these sell out)
  • Winter branch & bulb arranging
  • New year "learn floristry" beginners series

Virtual workshops, real flowers

Can't get to Stratford? We post a seasonal kit — British-grown stems, snips guide and mechanics — and teach the same class live online. Popular with scattered hen parties, teams, and brides planning their DIY wedding flowers from a distance.
