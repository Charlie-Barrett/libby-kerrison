Where workshops meet weddings

Make your own wedding flowers. Really.

For brides and couples who want to be taught, not just sold to: learn to construct seasonal arrangements with your own hands, then let our growers deliver your wedding's stems fresh from the field. Meaningful, budget-kind, and genuinely yours.

The programme

How the bridal programme works

Consultation & season check

We map your date against the growing calendar, agree the look, and plan what Anna will grow for you.

Learning sessions

One or more workshops — in person at Dolphin House or virtual with posted kits — covering conditioning, hand-tied bouquets, jars and table arrangements, buttonholes.

Wedding-week delivery

Your stems arrive cut-to-order, conditioned and bucket-ready, with a written quantities-and-care plan.

The making day

Honest guidance

Is DIY right for you?

We'll tell you straight: a 120-guest wedding with arch installations is not a first-timer's project — for that, take our full wedding service or a hybrid (we do the big pieces, you make the tables). But bouquets, jars and buttonholes for an intimate wedding? Completely learnable, and the workshops exist to prove it.

  • Best for: jam-jar tables, hand-tied bouquets, relaxed venues
  • Hybrid option: we build installations, you make the rest
  • Hen parties double brilliantly as practice sessions
